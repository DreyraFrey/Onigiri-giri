Review (Spaced Repetition)

Practice quizzes and matching games, then use Review to strengthen memory.

  • Long-term — items you know well come back after days. Items you miss come back sooner.
  • Short-term — inside one session, correct items reappear a little later before they’re finished (like Pimsleur).
  • The Vocab Exam is separate and not part of this system.
